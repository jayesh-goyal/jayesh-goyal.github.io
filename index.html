// CYBER FRAUD ALERTS — INDIA

Latest cybercrime trends and warnings you should know about

🚨 UPI Fraud on the Rise

Fraudsters are posing as bank officials and tricking victims into sharing OTPs. Never share OTP with anyone — banks never ask for OTPs on calls.

June 2026 | Source: CERT-In

⚠️ Fake Job Offer Scam

Cyber criminals are sending fake job offers via WhatsApp and Telegram asking for registration fees. Legitimate companies never charge fees for job applications.

June 2026 | Source: Cyber Cell

🔴 SIM Swap Fraud Alert

Attackers are submitting fake SIM swap requests to telecom operators to steal OTPs. Contact your telecom immediately if your SIM stops working unexpectedly.

June 2026 | Source: TRAI

⚠️ Fake Investment Apps

Multiple fake investment apps promising high returns are circulating on WhatsApp. Only use SEBI registered investment platforms.

June 2026 | Source: RBI

🚨 Aadhaar OTP Fraud

Fraudsters claiming to be UIDAI officials are calling citizens asking for Aadhaar OTPs. UIDAI never calls citizens asking for OTPs.

June 2026 | Source: UIDAI
